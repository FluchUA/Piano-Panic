## Piano-Panic

## Overview
Piano-Panic is an interactive, social music game built right into Reddit. It allows users to channel their inner composer, create unique tracks, and share them with the community. Players can browse the subreddit to listen to and rate masterpieces created by others, earn musical currency called Notes, unlock rare instruments, and climb the ranks from a simple Whistler to a legendary Grand Maestro.

Inspired heavily by the vintage, rubber-hose aesthetic of Cuphead, the game combines music creation with a nostalgic cartoon vibe.

## How It Works
1. Create: Open the studio and use the interactive keyboard to record your own custom melody.
2. Save & Publish: Save the track to your personal Vinyl Records collection and publish it directly to the subreddit as an interactive post.
3. Listen & Rate: Browse the community feed, listen to tracks from other creators, and rate them from 1 to 10. Rating unlocks only after listening to keep it fair!
4. Earn & Spend: Earn Notes for publishing and rating. Spend your wealth at The Music Emporium to buy new instruments (Organ, Retro, Synth Piano, Electro) or extend your maximum track duration.
5. Flex Your Rank: Accumulate lifetime Notes to level up your musician status and compete for the ultimate Grand Maestro title.

## Features
* Built-in Music Editor: A fully functional keyboard with sharp/flat keys, octave switching, and sustain pedal support.
* The Music Emporium: A progression shop featuring distinct instrument sound fonts and time upgrades.
* Anti-Spam Rating System: A lock-out mechanic that ensures users actually listen to the track before casting their vote.
* Live Analytics: Track total unique listeners and real-time average community scores on your published tracks.
* Musician Ranks: 5 unlockable tiers (Whistler, Street Busker, Jazz Cat, Virtuoso, Grand Maestro) to track your legacy.

## Behind the Art
Every visual asset, interface element, and frame of animation in ToonTune was created entirely from scratch by me. Heavily inspired by the iconic art style of Cuphead, this project was my first experience with hand-drawn animation, brought to life completely using Krita.
